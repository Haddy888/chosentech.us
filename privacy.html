What the forms collect

Quote forms collect name, work email, phone, company, service interest, device range, metro, and your message. Career forms also collect a resume filename and, once the API exists, the file itself. Until the webhook is configured, the preview script does not transmit that data to Chosen Technology.

How it is used

Sales and recruiting follow-up. We do not sell form data. Career files, when the pipeline is live, should go to a quarantine bucket and be scanned before a recruiter can open them.
